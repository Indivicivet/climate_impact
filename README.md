# Climate impact calculator

Open `index.html` in a browser. Choose the closest preset in each row, then open **More** to adjust it. Compare your estimate with a modelled lifestyle or a saved profile. Saved profiles stay in your browser.

- **Home energy.** Pick a whole-home preset or enter your dwelling type, thermostat, outdoor climate and heating system. Appliance electricity can come from a preset or your bill.
- **Goods and services.** Use personal spending in pounds sterling or the equivalent, recent device purchases, parcels and your share of hotel nights.
- **Shared services.** Pick where you live. The estimate allocates an average share of public services and investment in buildings and other assets.
- **AI.** Enter processed text tokens, including input and output. The plan examples change volume. The energy and hardware rates are separate.
- **Donations.** Set an annual amount and a project cost per tonne. The pie shows your gross footprint and, if you enter a donation, the estimated net amount beneath it.

Hover or focus a pie slice to see its annual tonnes and share. The country comparisons use the same calculator with illustrative inputs informed by available statistics. The totals and slices are model estimates.

See [MAGIC_NUMBERS.md](MAGIC_NUMBERS.md) for the conversion factors, worked examples and sources.

To publish on GitHub Pages, set the publishing source to the root of the branch containing `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` and `model.js`. The calculator runs in the browser.
