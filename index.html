Method & sources

Estimates are in tonnes of CO₂e per person per year. We add emissions from food, travel, home energy, purchases, shared services and AI use. Flight estimates include an allowance for warming effects at altitude.

Home heating and cooling use the dwelling type, thermostat and local temperature range. Shopping and leisure use spending, with a fixed allowance for devices and telecom. Travel covers return flights and hotel nights. Shared services are an estimate for where you live. AI plan examples set token volume.

Many factors use UK data, including shared resources and home construction. Estimates for other regions can be less representative; see the calculations and sources for details.

The comparison pies show modelled lifestyles. The outer ring breaks down each category. With a donation, the pie centre shows estimated net emissions at the price per tonne you choose, with gross emissions underneath.
