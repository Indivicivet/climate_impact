Method & sources

Estimates are in tonnes of CO₂e per person per year. We add emissions from food, travel, home energy, purchases, shared services and AI use. Flight estimates include an allowance for warming effects at altitude.

Home heating uses the dwelling type, thermostat and local temperature range. Purchases use spending, devices, deliveries and hotel nights. Shared services are an average for where you live. AI plan buttons change token volume, with a separate setting for energy per million tokens.

The comparison pies show modelled lifestyles. The outer ring shows how each category breaks down. Donations leave the gross footprint intact and add an estimated net figure at the price per tonne you choose.
